02 / RATING GUIDE

Know what to rate

Separate delivery, content, conversational flow and technical audio quality.

IQ-FOCUSED

Utility first

Utility > NEA > AQ

EQ-FOCUSED

Naturalness first

NEA > Utility > AQ

HYBRID

Scenario skills first

Use the skills being tested.

NEANaturalness / Engagement / Aesthetics

Human-like delivery, emotion, expression, pronunciation, tone.

UtilityHelpfulness & accuracy

Relevance, completeness, factual accuracy and instruction-following.

CDConversational Dynamics

Turn-taking, interruptions, responsiveness and conversational flow.

AQAudio Quality

Technical artifacts: warbling, distortion, ticks, pops and glitches.

Task SuccessPass / fail gate

Whether the model achieved the user's main objective.

Error ClusterRecognized error patterns

Apply the official definitions; do not force a category.

NOTE

A catastrophic failure can outweigh the normal priority. Minor errors do not automatically mean failure.

Make evidence specific

Name the model, turn and timestamp when possible.

Example format: MB, T4, 0:11 — audible warbling.

Avoid unsupported claims

Do not assume vocal tone or audio quality from a transcript. Report only what actually occurred.

Explain the trade-off

Connect the overall preference to scenario priority. Explain why the evidence matters.
