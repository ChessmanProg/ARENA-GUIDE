06 / LEARNING HUB

Quick reference.

Open a concept to review what it means, then close it when you are done.

NEA — Naturalness, engagement and aesthetics

Listen for natural speech, emotional fit, pronunciation and expressive delivery. Text can show wording, but it cannot prove vocal tone.

Utility — Relevance and accuracy

Check whether the response actually helps, follows instructions, covers the goal and avoids unsupported factual claims.

CD — Conversational dynamics

Listen for how turns are exchanged, whether a model responds at the right time and whether interruptions or long pauses affect the interaction.

AQ — Audio quality

Listen for technical sound defects such as warbling, ticks, pops, distortion or glitches. Do not confuse intentional vocal expression with a technical artifact.

Task success — Pass or fail

Judge the main objective according to the official criteria. A minor error is not automatically a failure. If evidence is missing, do not claim it was observed.

Error clusters — Patterns of errors

Use the official category definitions and supporting examples. Do not assume a category merely because a single phrase sounds repetitive.

Fair comparison — Keep conditions similar

Use comparable topics, follow-ups, energy and length. Respect the scenario requirements, including any specified number of turns.

Factual verification — Check claims

Use reliable sources where the project allows or requires verification. Distinguish a missing source from a confirmed factual error.
